I make things that feel good to look at.

I operate at the intersection of architecture, visual systems and fine art.

​

With a Master degree specialized in Interior Architecture, my background spans spatial environments, visual identity design, and digital media.

​

Rather than treating these fields as separate, I focus on their synergy.
My most extensive project, Brutalizam i renesansa (2018 - present), embodies this multi-faceted mindset, combining the structural rigor of architecture, the clarity of graphic design, and the tactile depth of visual art.

My core focus is atmosphere as the primary identity of space.
