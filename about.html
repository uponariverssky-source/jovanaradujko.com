Jovana Radujko, architect and artist from New Belgrade.

Methodology: Inside-Out
My work is an analytical deconstruction of the built environment. I do not illustrate buildings - I document the structural logic and atmospheric precision of spatial volumes.

​

Archive: Brutalizam i Renesansa
A systematic study of urban landscapes through high-precision manual drafting. The project’s opus consists of over 200 architectural records, including a 2-meter axonometric map of New Belgrade.

​

Currently working on: Belgrade 360°
A technical exploration of nocturnal urban panorama, observed from the apex of the Western City Gate.
