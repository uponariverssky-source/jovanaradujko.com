Jovana Radujko, architect and artist from New Belgrade.

My work centres on the exploration of space and architectural atmospheres. Since 2018, through the project Brutalizam i Renesansa (Brutalism and Renaissance), I explore my personal relationship with the built environment, inscribing it through freehand drawings. I deal with questions of structure, the relationship between solid and void, light and shadow. The project's opus includes over 200 drawings, including a hand-drawn axonometric map of New Belgrade. I am currently working on Belgrade 360°, driven by the idea of drawing the observer into a subjective experience of the nocturnal panorama of the city from the top of the Western City Gate of Belgrade.
